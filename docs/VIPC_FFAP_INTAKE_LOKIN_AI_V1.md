## Technology

LOKIN Adaptive Driver Decision Intelligence

## Technical summary

LOKIN is developing a confidence-calibrated mobility decision architecture that distinguishes authoritative location anchors from temporary inertial estimates, models uncertainty, reduces ambiguous road matching and false rerouting, and conditions explainable human-authorized work decisions on evidence quality.

## Current engineering evidence

- iOS Swift and Android Kotlin location/sensor-fusion packages
- bounded dead-reckoning and uncertainty model
- online HMM-style road matching
- structured navigation telemetry and calibration data contracts
- SEAL explainable decision records with confidence, reason and uncertainty codes
- physical-device field-calibration protocol

## Unresolved R&D

- commercially useful dead-reckoning horizon on commodity phones
- parallel-road and urban-canyon discrimination under degraded GNSS
- confidence calibration for safe reroute decisions
- whether uncertainty-aware operational recommendations outperform simpler baselines
- battery/reliability envelope on representative physical devices
